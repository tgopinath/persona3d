# Persona3D Studio

**Pitch:** Photos of a person in, a rigged and animated 3D character out.

**Description:** A guided six-step wizard takes photos from several angles, checks each one for resolution, lighting, blur and face visibility in the browser, then runs a staged generation job (geometry, textures, rig, animations) that survives refreshes and can be retried from the failed stage. The studio has a 3D viewer with cameras and lighting presets, 26 animation clips with sequences and a prompt-to-motion planner, customisation, and GLB/GLTF/OBJ/PNG/MP4 export. Privacy is designed in: consent before upload, private storage behind expiring signed links, per-user rate limits, deletion and retention controls, and a public-demo guard that refuses photos on a shared deployment. The built-in Demo provider is clearly labelled and builds a parametric character, not a likeness; Meshy is wired for real reconstruction.
